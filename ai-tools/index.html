Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before choosing an AI service.

Are these AI tools free?

Some services offer free access or a free tier, but limits and included features differ. Check each provider's current pricing and terms before use.

Which AI tool should I start with?

For general questions and drafting, try a general-purpose assistant. For studying from your own documents, consider a source-based research tool. For coding or image creation, choose a tool built for that task.

Can I trust every answer generated by AI?

No. AI can make mistakes or omit important context. Verify facts, citations, calculations and important decisions against reliable original sources.

Is it safe to upload private documents?

Review the service's privacy settings and data policies first. Avoid uploading passwords, payment details, confidential work data or personal information that is not needed for the task.
