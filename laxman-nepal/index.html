About Laxman Nepal

A creator, educator and hands-on digital builder.

This is the official biography and reference page for Laxman Nepal.

Laxman Nepal is a Nepali technology content creator, YouTuber, computer educator and digital builder. He is known for creating Nepali-language technology tutorials and for developing practical digital resources through his website and public software projects.

His interests include smartphones and computers, AI tools, online productivity, web applications, creator workflows and accessible digital education. His approach is practical: learn a tool, test it, explain what works, identify its limitations and make the knowledge easier for other people to use.

His educational background includes +2 Management/Commerce and a one-year Computer Applications diploma. He has worked as a computer instructor and IT trainer, helping learners develop practical computer skills. He has also gained professional experience in financial services and exchange operations, including cashiering and head-teller responsibilities.

Alongside content creation, he develops and maintains web projects, browser-based tools, data dashboards and Nepal-focused digital utilities. His long-term direction is to connect useful content, software, education and community into one coherent digital ecosystem.
