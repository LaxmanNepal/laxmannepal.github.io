About Laxman Nepal

Technology, education and building useful things.

This page is the official biographical profile for Laxman Nepal and connects his website, creator channel and public work.

Laxman Nepal is a Nepali technology content creator and YouTuber who publishes practical tutorials and explainers about smartphones, computers, AI tools, apps and digital productivity. His primary content language is Nepali, helping people understand and use technology in everyday life.

Alongside content creation, he builds and maintains web projects, browser-based tools, dashboards and Nepal-focused digital resources. His work combines hands-on experimentation, clear instruction and practical problem-solving.

His education includes +2 Management/Commerce and a one-year Computer Applications diploma. He has worked as a computer teacher and IT trainer, and has professional experience in financial-service and foreign-exchange operations.

His current creative focus is building a connected digital platform around Nepali technology education, AI-assisted workflows, useful web tools and creator resources.
