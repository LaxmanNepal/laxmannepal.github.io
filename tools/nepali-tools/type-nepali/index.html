How to type

  • Type phonetically: namaste → नमस्ते, mero desh → मेरो देश.
  • Press Space or Enter to accept the highlighted suggestion; use ↑/↓ to select and Tab to cycle. Press Shift + Enter for a new line.
  • Wrap English words in braces, such as {'{'}Laxman{'}'}, to keep them unchanged.
  • Use Ctrl + Z to undo and Ctrl + Y to redo.

Your draft is saved only in this browser. Google suggestions are off by default; enable them only if you want to send the current unfinished word to Google. Voice recognition may use your browser's speech provider. Drafts, named documents and learned typing choices stay in this browser. Basic typing works offline, but online suggestions and speech recognition may not.

About this Nepali Unicode typing tool

This free browser-based editor helps you type Nepali Unicode with a Romanized keyboard, choose alternate word suggestions, and copy or download your writing. Local transliteration works without an account; optional online suggestions can improve word choices when connected.

Spelling and context affect transliteration, so review names and less-common words before publishing.

Frequently asked questions

Is my Nepali typing text uploaded?

No. Drafts and named documents stay in your browser. Google suggestions are off by default; if you enable them, only the current unfinished word is sent to Google. Browser speech recognition may use a speech service.

Can I use the Nepali typing tool offline?

After you first open the page while online and its service worker cache is installed, local typing and Romanized conversion can work offline. Google suggestions and speech recognition may be unavailable.

Can I save more than one document?

Yes. Open Documents, templates & editing tools to create and manage up to 30 named documents stored in this browser. You can also download your text as a .txt file.

Is Traditional keyboard mode a complete standard layout?

Not yet. The current direct-key mapping is partial, and the page labels it as partial rather than claiming full keyboard-layout coverage.

Can I convert old Preeti-font text?

Yes. Use the Preeti ↔ Unicode Converter from the Nepali Tools directory.
