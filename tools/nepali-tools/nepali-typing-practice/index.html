How scoring works

  • WPM: correct graphemes divided by five, then divided by elapsed minutes.
  • Accuracy: correct graphemes divided by the number of graphemes you typed.
  • Errors: wrong-position and extra graphemes. Devanagari combining marks are grouped using the browser's grapheme segmenter where available.
  • Privacy: the passage, timer and score are processed locally and are not saved or uploaded by this page.

Frequently asked questions

How is typing speed calculated?

WPM is correct graphemes divided by five and then divided by elapsed minutes. CPM is correct graphemes per minute.

Are my practice scores uploaded?

No. The page calculates the passage, timer and score in your browser and does not upload or save them.

Can I practice my own text?

Yes. Edit the target passage before starting. Changing the passage or duration resets the attempt.
