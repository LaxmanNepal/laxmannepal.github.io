How scoring works

  • WPM: correct graphemes divided by five, then divided by elapsed minutes.
  • Accuracy: correct graphemes divided by the number of graphemes you typed.
  • Errors: wrong-position and extra graphemes. Devanagari combining marks are grouped using the browser's grapheme segmenter where available.
  • Privacy: the passage, timer and score are processed locally and are not saved or uploaded by this page.
