Preeti ↔ Unicode Converter

Convert legacy Preeti-encoded text into Nepali Unicode, or convert Unicode Nepali back to Preeti for older documents. Conversion runs locally in your browser.

Paste text to preview the conversion.

Local conversion only: your text is not uploaded or saved by this page. Keep the original until you have reviewed the converted result.

Before converting

  • Preeti is a legacy font encoding: the text may look like English letters until the correct font is applied.
  • For Preeti → Unicode, the input should be Preeti-encoded. Ordinary English inside the same text may also be converted, so review the preview carefully.
  • Different legacy Nepali fonts can use different key maps. This converter targets Preeti; it does not claim lossless support for Kantipur, PCS Nepali, or every custom font.
  • For normal Romanized typing, use the Nepali typing tool.

Frequently asked questions

What is Preeti text?

Preeti is a legacy Nepali font encoding where Latin-key characters can display Devanagari glyphs when the Preeti font is applied.

Can I convert Unicode back to Preeti?

Yes. Choose Unicode Nepali → Preeti, then copy or download the encoded result and apply the Preeti font in the target document.

Does the converter preserve English words in Preeti mode?

The current Preeti → Unicode direction converts all characters in the input. Ordinary English text may also change, so review the preview and keep your original.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Conversion runs locally in the browser and the page does not save or upload your text.
