ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

Useful digital tools, explained clearly.

Laxman Nepal is an independent technology project created by Laxman Nepal. It brings together browser-based utilities, practical technology tutorials and guides intended to help people complete everyday digital tasks with less friction.

The site is developed and maintained as an evolving collection. Features and guides may change as they are tested, improved or retired.

What you will find here

  • Browser tools: small utilities for common tasks, with instructions where a task needs extra context.
  • Technology and AI guides: practical explanations and workflows, including steps, requirements and limitations where relevant.
  • Creator resources: guides and utilities for YouTube and digital content workflows.
  • Nepal-focused resources: selected local digital information and tools, where sources and update timing matter.

How content is approached

The aim is to make each page useful on its own—not simply to list a tool or repeat a product description. Guides should explain the problem, show the process, identify important limitations and link to primary sources when appropriate. For changing information such as prices, availability or software features, check the stated source and date before relying on it.

Tools are provided for general information and productivity. Please independently verify important financial, legal, medical or other high-stakes decisions.

Feedback and corrections

If a tool is broken, a guide is unclear or information needs correcting, please get in touch. Specific feedback helps prioritize improvements.
