🚀 How to Use This Tool:

  • Select File Type: Choose what you're looking for (Movies, Music, Books, etc.)
  • Choose Search Engine: Pick your preferred search engine
  • Enter Keywords: Type your search terms (e.g., movie title, software name)
  • Click Search: Results will open in a new tab
  • Download: Look for direct download links in the search results

💡 Search Tips:

  • Use dots instead of spaces (e.g., "The.Blacklist.S01" instead of "The Blacklist S01")
  • Include season and episode numbers for TV series
  • For software, include version numbers
  • Add "download" or "free" to your search terms
  • Try different search engines if you don't find what you need
