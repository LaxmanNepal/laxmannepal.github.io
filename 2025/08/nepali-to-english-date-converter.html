Understanding Nepali (Bikram Sambat) Calendar

The Bikram Sambat (BS) calendar is the official calendar of Nepal. Key facts about this calendar system:

  • Starts in 57 BC (making the current year 2080 BS)
  • New Year begins in mid-April (approximately April 13-15)
  • Has 12 months with varying days (29-32 days per month)
  • Used for all official purposes in Nepal

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this converter?

Our converter uses official Nepali calendar data and is accurate for all dates between 1990 BS and 2100 BS. For historical dates beyond this range, slight variations may occur.

Why do some BS months have 32 days?

The Bikram Sambat calendar has months with varying lengths (29-32 days) to keep in sync with the solar year. This is different from the Gregorian calendar which has more consistent month lengths.

Can I convert dates before 1990 BS?

While our tool focuses on 1990-2100 BS for maximum accuracy, the conversion algorithms can work for earlier dates with potentially decreasing precision the further back you go.
