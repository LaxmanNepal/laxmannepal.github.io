Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains how LaxmanNepal.com.np handles information when you browse the website or use its public tools. The site is maintained by Laxman Nepal. For questions, use the contact page.

Some tools may process input directly in your browser. Do not enter passwords, private documents or other sensitive information into a tool unless its page clearly explains why the information is needed and how it is handled.

Such services may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to measure visits, prevent abuse, or select and measure ads. If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize or measure advertising, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements and Google's policies. Third-party providers process data under their own policies.

For information about Google's advertising data practices, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage Google ad personalization at Google Ads Settings.

When advertising or analytics features are added or changed, this policy should be updated to describe the services actually in use. Browser settings may let you block or delete cookies, although some features may then behave differently.

External links and embedded services

Pages may link to third-party websites, apps, videos or services. Laxman Nepal does not control their content, cookies or privacy practices. Review their policies before submitting information to them.

Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been submitted to the site, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the site or its services change. The latest version will be published at this URL with its updated date.
