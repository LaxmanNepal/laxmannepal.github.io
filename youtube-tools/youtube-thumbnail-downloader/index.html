How to Download YouTube Video Thumbnails

Downloading thumbnail images from YouTube videos is quick and effortless using our free web app. Follow these 3 simple steps:

  1. Copy YouTube Link: Open YouTube on your web browser or app and copy the URL of the video or Short.
  2. Paste URL: Paste the link into the input field above and click Get Thumbnails.
  3. Choose Resolution & Download: Pick your preferred resolution (1080p, HD, or SD) and click the Download button.

Supported YouTube Video URL Formats

Our extractor supports all official YouTube link formats including:

  • Standard links: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID
  • Shortened URLs: https://youtu.be/VIDEO_ID
  • YouTube Shorts: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/VIDEO_ID
  • Embedded URLs: https://www.youtube.com/embed/VIDEO_ID

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this YouTube Thumbnail Downloader completely free?

What thumbnail resolutions can I download?
You can download Full HD (1280x720), High Quality (480x360), Standard Quality (640x480), and Medium Quality (320x180) images.
Why is the 1080p / 4K thumbnail missing for a video?
YouTube automatically generates maximum resolution thumbnails (maxresdefault) only if the creator uploaded a high-resolution custom thumbnail. If missing, our tool seamlessly highlights the highest available alternative.
