The **Redmi 14C and Redmi 13C** target buyers who want a large screen, long battery life and a low price. The newer model is not simply better in every area, so this comparison focuses on what you actually get.

## Display comparison

Redmi 14C has a 6.88-inch display with up to a 120Hz refresh rate. Redmi 13C has a 6.74-inch display with up to 90Hz. If you watch a lot of video or prefer a large screen, Redmi 14C has the obvious advantage.

## Performance

Redmi 14C uses MediaTek Helio G81-Ultra, while Redmi 13C uses MediaTek Helio G85. Neither is a dedicated gaming chipset, but both are designed for everyday apps, social media and casual gaming.

## Camera

Redmi 14C has a 50MP AI dual-camera system and 13MP front camera. Redmi 13C also uses a 50MP main camera and has an 8MP front camera. For normal daylight photos, both can be practical budget choices.

## Battery

Redmi 14C has a 5,160mAh battery and 18W charging. Redmi 13C has a 5,000mAh battery and supports 18W PD charging, although Xiaomi notes that a 10W adapter may be included depending on region.

## Which one should you buy?

Choose **Redmi 14C** if you want the larger 6.88-inch screen, up to 120Hz and a slightly larger battery. Choose **Redmi 13C** if it is significantly cheaper or you find the 8GB+256GB configuration at a good price.

## Verdict

For most new buyers, **Redmi 14C is the more attractive specification package**. But the Redmi 13C remains a good value option when discounts make the price gap large. Always verify the exact Nepal configuration before buying.
