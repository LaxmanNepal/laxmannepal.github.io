If camera quality is your top priority, a Xiaomi phone under Rs. 30,000 can give you high-resolution photos without moving into flagship pricing. But a **108MP label is not enough** to determine which camera is better.

## Which Xiaomi phone has the best camera under Rs. 30,000?

The **Redmi Note 13** is a strong candidate because it combines a 108MP main camera with an 8MP ultra-wide and 2MP macro camera. Its camera system is more flexible than a phone with only a high-resolution main sensor.

The **Redmi 13** also has a 108MP main camera and supports 3X lossless in-sensor zoom. It is much cheaper, which makes it attractive for buyers who want detailed daylight photos without spending close to Rs. 30,000.

## Does more megapixels mean better photos?

Not necessarily. Sensor size, lens quality, image processing, exposure, dynamic range and stabilization can matter just as much. A 108MP camera can produce excellent daylight detail but still struggle in difficult night scenes if the rest of the imaging system is limited.

## Which is better for portraits?

Both phones can create portrait-style photos, but the final result depends on software processing and lighting. For natural-looking portraits, use good light, keep the subject separated from the background and avoid excessive digital zoom.

## Which is better for video?

Before buying, check the exact recording modes supported by the Nepal variant. Buyers often compare still-photo megapixels while ignoring video stabilization, resolution and frame-rate support.

## Camera verdict

If you want **maximum camera flexibility**, Redmi Note 13 is the stronger choice. If you want **108MP detail at a lower price**, Redmi 13 is excellent value. For either phone, look at real sample photos and the exact variant before purchasing.
