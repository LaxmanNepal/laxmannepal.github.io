If you want a **Xiaomi phone with an AMOLED display under Rs. 30,000**, the Redmi Note 13 is one of the clearest models to investigate.

## Why is AMOLED important?

AMOLED panels can produce deep blacks because individual pixels can be controlled independently. This can make movies, photos and dark-mode interfaces look more impressive than a conventional LCD.

The Redmi Note 13 has a 6.67-inch FHD+ AMOLED display and 120Hz refresh rate. Xiaomi Nepal lists it at Rs. 24,999 for 6GB+128GB and Rs. 27,999 for 8GB+256GB.

## Is 120Hz better than 90Hz?

For scrolling and supported content, 120Hz can look smoother. However, refresh rate is only one part of display quality. Panel type, resolution, brightness, color accuracy and touch response also matter.

## Should you choose AMOLED over a bigger LCD?

That depends on your use. If you watch Netflix, YouTube and social media frequently, AMOLED can be a major quality-of-life improvement. If you mostly need a large screen for reading and battery-focused use, a bigger LCD can still make sense.

## What else does Redmi Note 13 offer?

The phone combines the AMOLED 120Hz panel with Snapdragon 685, a 108MP main camera, 5,000mAh battery, 33W charging and up to 8GB+256GB storage in the Nepal listing.

## Verdict

For buyers specifically searching for **AMOLED under Rs. 30,000**, Redmi Note 13 is a logical starting point. Compare current pricing against newer Xiaomi and Redmi models before purchasing, because the best value changes whenever discounts or new models arrive.
