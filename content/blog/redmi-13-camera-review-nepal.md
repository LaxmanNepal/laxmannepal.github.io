The **Redmi 13** puts a 108MP main camera into a relatively affordable phone. That sounds impressive, but the more useful question is: **how does the camera fit real everyday photography?**

## What camera does Redmi 13 have?

Redmi 13 uses a 108MP main camera with a 1/1.67-inch sensor and f/1.75 aperture, plus a 2MP auxiliary camera. It also has a 13MP selfie camera with a soft-light ring.

## Is 108MP useful?

In bright light, the high-resolution sensor can capture a lot of detail. The phone can also use in-sensor zoom for up to 3X lossless-style cropping. But high-resolution modes can create larger files, and good lighting remains important.

## How is night photography?

A larger sensor can help collect more light, but budget phones still have limits. Keep the phone steady, use night mode when appropriate and avoid unnecessary digital zoom in dark scenes.

## Is it good for social media?

Yes. The main camera is capable of producing detailed photos that work well for Facebook, Instagram and messaging. The 13MP front camera is also useful for selfies and video calls.

## Camera verdict

Redmi 13 is not a replacement for a flagship camera phone, but at its Nepal price it offers impressive resolution and useful everyday flexibility. If camera is your only priority and your budget is close to Rs. 30,000, compare it against Redmi Note models before deciding.
