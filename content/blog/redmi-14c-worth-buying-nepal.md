The Redmi 14C is aimed at buyers who want a large display and strong battery without crossing the Rs. 20,000 range.

## How much does Redmi 14C cost?

Xiaomi Nepal lists 4GB+128GB at Rs. 13,999, 6GB+128GB at Rs. 15,999 and 8GB+256GB at Rs. 18,999.

## What is special about the display?

It has a 6.88-inch display with a refresh rate of up to 120Hz. For a budget phone, the combination of large size and high refresh rate makes scrolling and video consumption more enjoyable.

## Is the battery good?

The 5,160mAh battery is one of the phone's strongest features. It supports 18W charging and Xiaomi lists up to 22 hours of video playback under its test conditions. Real-world results will vary.

## Is it good for gaming?

The Helio G81-Ultra can handle everyday games and casual gaming, but this is still a budget device. People who play demanding games for long periods should consider stronger hardware.

## Which variant should you buy?

If your budget allows it, **8GB+256GB at Rs. 18,999** is the most comfortable configuration for long-term storage. If your usage is light, 6GB+128GB can save money.

## Verdict

Redmi 14C is worth considering if your priorities are **large screen, high refresh rate, big battery and low price**. It is not the best choice for every buyer, but it is a sensible budget Redmi for everyday use.
