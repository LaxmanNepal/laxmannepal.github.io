## Is 8GB RAM actually necessary?

Not for everyone. If you mainly call, browse, watch videos and use a few social apps, 6GB can be sufficient. But heavy multitasking, photo editing and keeping many apps in memory can benefit from additional physical RAM.

## Is 256GB better than 128GB?

For most long-term users, yes. Modern apps, 4K videos, photos and offline downloads consume storage quickly. A 256GB phone reduces the need to constantly delete files.

## Which one is better value?

If your goal is simply **maximum storage for minimum money**, Redmi 13 8GB+256GB is hard to ignore. If you also want AMOLED and a more premium display, Redmi Note 13 8GB+256GB is the better overall package.

## Verdict

Choose **Redmi 13 8GB+256GB** for value. Choose **Redmi Note 13 8GB+256GB** for a more premium display and camera experience. Check the latest official price before buying because promotional pricing can change the comparison.
