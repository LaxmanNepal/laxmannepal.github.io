Students usually need a phone that can handle online classes, documents, messaging, social media, video calls and entertainment without consuming the entire budget.

## What should students prioritize?

A good student phone needs reliable battery life, enough storage, a comfortable display and smooth everyday performance. Camera quality is useful for scanning documents and taking notes, but it does not need to be the most advanced feature.

## Redmi 13 for students

Redmi 13 is attractive because its 8GB+256GB variant is listed by Xiaomi Nepal at Rs. 20,999. It has a 6.79-inch FHD+ 90Hz display, 108MP camera, 5,030mAh battery and 33W charging.

That gives a student plenty of storage for PDFs, lecture recordings, photos and apps while leaving budget for accessories.

## Redmi Note 13 for students

If the budget can reach Rs. 27,999, Redmi Note 13 8GB+256GB adds an AMOLED 120Hz display. This can make long reading and video sessions more enjoyable, although the more expensive model is not necessary for every student.

## Redmi 14C for students

Redmi 14C starts at Rs. 13,999 and offers a 6.88-inch display, up to 120Hz refresh rate and 5,160mAh battery. It is a strong option when budget is the main concern.

## Which should you buy?

For most students, **Redmi 13 8GB+256GB** is a very balanced starting point. Choose Redmi Note 13 if you value AMOLED more than saving money. Choose Redmi 14C if your budget is much tighter.

## Final advice

Do not spend your entire budget on a phone if you also need a laptop, headphones or study accessories. A slightly cheaper Redmi with 256GB storage can be a smarter student purchase than a more expensive phone with features you rarely use.
