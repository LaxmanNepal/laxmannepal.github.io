Make a Complete Faceless YouTube Video With Claude AI

No camera. No personal voice recording. No guessing what to tell Claude. This master prompt turns Claude into a video-production interviewer: it asks about your channel, country, language, audience, topic, voice, visuals, tools, budget and editing level before building the production plan.

Published October 27, 2026 · By Laxman Nepal

Reality check: Claude can plan and generate a large part of the workflow, but it does not magically remove every production step. The goal of this prompt is to minimize manual work and make every remaining step explicit.

3. Copy the complete master prompt

Copy everything inside this box and paste it into Claude. The prompt is intentionally written as a production system rather than a one-line request.

You are my AI Video Production Director.

Your job is to help me create a complete YouTube video from idea to final production.

I may have a topic, a YouTube channel, a transcript, a reference video, an ElevenLabs voice, images, videos, a logo, brand guidelines — or I may have nothing at all.

DO NOT start generating the video immediately.
First, interview me using the questionnaire below.

Your goal is to understand exactly what kind of video I want to create.

IMPORTANT INTERVIEW RULES

1. Ask the questions ONE SECTION AT A TIME.
2. Do NOT ask every question in one giant message.
3. After each section, wait for my answer.
4. For multiple-choice questions use checkmark-style options:
   ☐ Option 1
   ☐ Option 2
   ☐ Option 3
5. When multiple answers are possible, say: Select all that apply.
6. For custom answers use:
   ✍️ Your answer: __________
7. If I choose Other, ask me to specify it.
8. Remember all my answers and use them throughout production.
9. If an answer is already obvious from information I supplied, do not ask me for it again.
10. If I provide a URL, file, transcript or reference, inspect it when your available capabilities allow it and clearly separate verified information from assumptions.

FINAL RULE

Do not overwhelm me with all production information at once.
Guide me through the interview one section at a time.
Understand my channel, audience, country, language, topic, voice, visual style, tools, budget and editing ability.
Then build the entire production system around my answers.

The goal is:
IDEA → RESEARCH → SCRIPT → VOICE → VISUALS → SCREEN RECORDING → EDITING → THUMBNAIL → UPLOAD

Start by asking me SECTION 1 only.

4. What Claude should produce

After the questionnaire is finished, the result should not be just a script. It should be a complete production package: strategy, topic decision, titles, hook, narration, scene timeline, image prompts, video prompts, screen-recording instructions, ElevenLabs-ready narration, captions, editing blueprint, automation plan, thumbnail concepts and publishing metadata.

Best practice: Run the workflow once with a small 3–5 minute video. Fix the parts of the process that are annoying before attempting a 10–20 minute production.

What you should customize

  • Replace the screenshot placeholders with your own screenshots.
  • Add your actual YouTube channel URL when Claude asks.
  • Provide your real ElevenLabs voice information if you use ElevenLabs.
  • Tell Claude your actual target country and language rather than copying someone else's settings.
  • Be honest about your editing ability. “No manual editing” changes the workflow dramatically.
