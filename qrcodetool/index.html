Frequently asked questions

Are these QR codes free?

Yes. This page provides QR generation and scanning without an account or subscription. Your device and browser may use data to load the page's public JavaScript libraries.

Do generated QR codes expire?

A QR code is a representation of the content you encoded. It does not expire by itself, but a website, Wi-Fi network, phone number or other destination can change or stop working.

What is the difference between PNG and SVG?

PNG is a widely compatible raster image suited to everyday use. SVG is a container format that can be placed in design tools, but this download embeds a raster QR image at the generated resolution. The PNG includes the optional center logo.

Why won't my QR code scan?

Use strong contrast, avoid very long content, keep the QR code large enough, and do not cover too much of it with a logo. Test the downloaded file before printing. Some camera apps may handle special formats differently.

How does password-protected QR work?

The content is encrypted in your browser using AES-GCM with a password-derived key. The QR does not contain the password. To read it, scan the QR with this tool and enter the same password. This is not a password gate on an ordinary website link; it protects the text stored inside the QR. If you lose the password, the content cannot be recovered.

Can I turn an image into a QR code?

QR codes store text, not full-size image files. To make a QR that opens an image, upload the image to a trusted host and encode its public image URL. You can also add a small center logo here. For privacy, do not upload sensitive images to public hosting.

Is scanning private?

Camera scanning happens in the browser after you grant permission. Always check decoded links before opening them; a QR code can point to a malicious website just like any other link.
