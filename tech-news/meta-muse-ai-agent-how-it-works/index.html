Meta Muse AI Agent: What It Is, How It Works and What Users Should Know

Meta's Muse is designed to do more than answer questions: it can take actions, use connected apps and work toward longer-term goals.

Laxman Nepal · Technology Writer & Creator

What is Meta Muse?

Meta introduced Muse as a personal AI agent built to take action on a user's behalf. Meta says Muse can handle tasks such as sending email, booking travel and turning saved content into practical plans. It is available through the Muse app and can also be used through WhatsApp.

How Muse differs from a chatbot

A chatbot mainly produces information in response to a prompt. An agent is designed to continue through multiple steps, interact with tools and complete an objective. That makes permissions, confirmation steps and activity logs much more important.

How its secure computer works

Meta says Muse runs inside a dedicated Muse Secure VM containing the agent and connected user data. A separate Sentinel agent is intended to control internet access, while sensitive actions such as sending an email or making a purchase require user confirmation.

What users should check

  • Which apps and accounts Muse can access.
  • Which actions require confirmation.
  • What the audit trail shows.
  • Whether the feature is available in your country.
  • How connected credentials and data are handled.

Why this matters

The important shift is from AI that gives instructions to AI that executes tasks. That could save time, but it also means users need clear control over permissions and a reliable way to review what an agent did.

Nepal availability

Meta says Muse is rolling out in the US on iOS, Android and muse.ai, with wider availability described as coming later. Nepal access should therefore be checked directly rather than assumed.
