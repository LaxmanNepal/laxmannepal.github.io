Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6: What the New Android Chip Means for On-Device AI

Qualcomm has introduced Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 and 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, with a strong focus on AI running directly on flagship phones.

Laxman Nepal · Technology Writer & Creator

What Qualcomm announced

Qualcomm introduced two flagship mobile platforms at Snapdragon Summit 2026. Both are designed around higher CPU and GPU performance plus more capable on-device AI processing.

The 2nm shift

The new platforms use a 2nm process and a new Oryon CPU design. Qualcomm's published figures should be treated as company claims until independent testing across retail devices is available.

Why on-device AI matters

Local processing can reduce latency and can keep some workloads on the phone rather than sending every request to a cloud service. It can also enable AI features when connectivity is limited.

The Extreme model

Qualcomm says the higher-end Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 can run a 30-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model locally. The practical result will depend on software optimization and the applications that support it.

What users should watch

The chip itself does not guarantee better AI on every phone. Watch for actual devices, sustained performance, battery impact, thermal behavior and which AI features manufacturers expose.

Nepal buying context

When these chips reach phones sold in Nepal, buyers should compare the exact regional model, network bands, warranty and software features rather than relying only on the processor name.
