How to Use AI More Privately: 10 Things to Check Before Sharing Data

A practical privacy checklist for using ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and other AI tools without unnecessarily exposing sensitive information.

LN
Laxman NepalTechnology Writer & Creator · Original editorial

Why AI privacy matters

AI assistants increasingly handle personal and work information. Privacy controls vary by provider, product, account and plan, so users should check the current settings instead of assuming every AI service handles data the same way.

10 checks before sharing information

  1. Remove passwords and authentication codes.
  2. Do not paste payment-card details.
  3. Remove unnecessary personal identifiers.
  4. Check whether chat history or activity can be used for service improvement.
  5. Review connected apps and permissions.
  6. Understand file retention and deletion controls.
  7. Use approved enterprise tools for confidential work.
  8. Verify links before opening AI-generated suggestions.
  9. Fact-check important answers.
  10. Delete sensitive chats or files when appropriate.

Use the minimum necessary data

You often do not need to upload an entire document. Replace private names with placeholders and provide only the section needed for the task.

Bottom line

Convenience should not mean sharing everything. The safest workflow is to minimize data first and then use the AI features you actually need.

Editorial note

Features, availability and menu names can change. Verify the current options shown by your device or service before following a step that could delete data or affect an account.
