How to Use AI for Meeting Notes: A Practical Workflow

Learn how AI can turn meeting recordings or rough notes into summaries, decisions and action lists while reducing privacy risks.

LN
Laxman NepalTechnology Writer & Creator · Original editorial

What AI meeting notes can do

AI meeting tools can transcribe speech, summarize discussions, identify action items and organize decisions. Results depend on audio quality, speaker separation and the tool.

Before recording

Check your workplace rules and local requirements for recording or transcribing people. Tell participants when required.

After the meeting

Ask the AI to separate decisions, action items, owners, deadlines, unresolved questions and important context.

Verify names and numbers

Transcription systems can confuse names, technical terms and numbers. Verify anything that will affect a customer, payment, deadline or business decision.

Protect sensitive information

Understand where recordings and transcripts are stored, who can access them and how long they are retained. Avoid unnecessary confidential information.

Useful prompt

Prompt: Turn these meeting notes into five sections: decisions, action items with owners, deadlines, unresolved questions, and a short summary. Do not invent missing information.

Editorial note

Features, availability and menu names can change. Verify the current options shown by your device or service before following a step that could delete data or affect an account.
